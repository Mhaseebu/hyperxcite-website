About HyperXcite

HyperXcite Game Studio is an independent mobile and VR games studio obsessed with one thing: the rush. We build tight, fast, replayable games that respect your time and reward your reflexes — no bloat, no filler, just pure adrenaline in your pocket.

Slice Escape, Traffic Puzzle: City Jam, Clear Stack, and Hopwise are all live now on Google Play, with iOS releases and more games on the way. Our first VR game, Balloon Keepy-Uppy, is coming to Meta Quest 2, 3 and 3S on the Meta Horizon Store.
