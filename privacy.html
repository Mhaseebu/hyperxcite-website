Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

HyperXcite Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates mobile games, including Slice Escape (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

Depending on the app and platform, we may collect:

  • Device & usage data — device type, OS version, crash logs, and in-game analytics (e.g. levels played, session length) used to improve gameplay and fix bugs.
  • Advertising identifiers — if the app shows ads, our ad partners may use advertising IDs to serve and measure ads.
  • Store & platform data — information provided by Google Play / Apple App Store, such as purchase confirmations for in-app purchases, if applicable.

We do not require account creation to play, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as real names, addresses, or payment details directly within the app.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our games
  • To diagnose technical issues and crashes
  • To display and measure advertising, where applicable
  • To understand aggregate player behavior and engagement

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, analytics providers, and advertising networks. These third parties may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Retention & Security

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above and take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your Choices

You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier through your device settings, and you can uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection associated with it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
