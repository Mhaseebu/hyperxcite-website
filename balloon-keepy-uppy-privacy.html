Balloon Keepy-Uppy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy is published by HyperXcite Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), the developer and publisher of Balloon Keepy-Uppy (the "App"), a virtual reality game for Meta Quest 2, Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S. It explains what information the App uses, why, and the choices you have.

HyperXcite Game Studio is the organization that publishes Balloon Keepy-Uppy on the Meta Horizon Store, and it owns and maintains this policy at hyperxcite.online.

Who We Are (Data Controller)

HyperXcite Game Studio
Opiskelijankatu 4A 94, Tampere, Finland
Email: haseebmuhammad75@gmail.com

Summary

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • Your scores, coins, progress and settings are saved only on your headset.
  • The App has no ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics trackers of our own, no accounts, no chat and no real-money purchases.
  • Meta verifies that you own the App and records your achievements, under Meta's own privacy policy.
  • Hand tracking and Mixed Reality data are processed on your headset only and are never recorded or sent by the App.

Information Saved on Your Headset

The App keeps a small save file on your headset so you can continue where you left off. It contains your best scores, coins, Party Mode stars, items unlocked in the in-game shop, achievement progress, Daily Challenge streak, and your settings (such as seated mode and volume). This file stays on your device. We never receive it.

Information Handled by Meta

The App uses the Meta Platform SDK for two features:

  • Ownership check (entitlement): when the App starts, Meta confirms that your Meta account owns the App. Meta requires this for apps on its store.
  • Achievements: when you unlock an achievement, Meta records it on your Meta account so it can appear on your profile.

For these features Meta uses your Meta account's user ID. This information is processed by Meta Platforms under the Meta Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your Meta user ID, name or other account details.

Hand Tracking, Controllers and Mixed Reality

To let you hit balloons, the App reads the position and movement of your hands or controllers in real time. This data is used only while you play, only on your headset, and is never stored or transmitted.

In Mixed Reality mode your headset shows your real room through passthrough. The passthrough image is produced by the Meta Quest system; the App does not access, record or transmit camera images of your surroundings.

Technical Data

The App is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive limited anonymous technical information, such as device model, operating system version and performance or crash statistics, under the Unity Privacy Policy. This does not include your name or Meta account.

Children's Privacy

Meta Quest accounts are for people aged 10 and over, with parental supervision options set by Meta. The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not hold any personal data about you. Uninstalling the App deletes its save file from your headset. Achievement data is kept by Meta with your Meta account and can be managed in your Meta account settings.

Your Rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). To make a request, email us at the address above; we will reply within 30 days.

Future Online Features

If a future update adds online features such as leaderboards, your Meta display name and scores would be shared with other players through Meta's services. We will update this policy before any such feature is released.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us
